## Reusable Component Implementation

All dynamically retrieved media items from the NASA Image and Video Library render through `<NasaAttribution />` (`src/components/NasaAttribution.tsx`), ensuring:
1. Prominent "Source: NASA" citation
2. Original archival center & photographer metadata
3. Direct outbound hyperlink to the official NASA record page
4. Educational non-endorsement disclaimer
